# Sega - Chihiro

Thumbnails for the Sega Chihiro games of [gameflix](https://wizzardsk.github.io), laid out as [libretro-thumbnails](https://github.com/libretro-thumbnails) (`Named_Snaps`, `Named_Titles`, `Named_Boxarts`, `Named_Logos`), named after the netboot images of the [ChihiroNetboot](https://archive.org/details/ChihiroNetboot) item.

MAME does not run Chihiro games, so the Arcade Database has only black or garbled screens for them. The pictures come from elsewhere:

- Snaps: the arcade games' Xbox and Wii ports from libretro-thumbnails (Crazy Taxi High Roller, OutRun 2, The House of the Dead 3: `Microsoft - Xbox`; Ghost Squad: `Nintendo - Wii`), and arcade screenshots from the [LaunchBox Games Database](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/) (Ollie King, Virtua Cop 3).
- Titles: arcade title screens from [Sega Retro](https://segaretro.org/) (OutRun 2, Ollie King) and the LaunchBox Games Database (Crazy Taxi High Roller, Virtua Cop 3); the ports' title screens for The House of the Dead 3 (Xbox, libretro-thumbnails) and Ghost Squad (Wii, Sega Retro).
- Boxarts: the arcade flyers from the [Arcade Database](https://adb.arcadeitalia.net/).
- Logos: libretro-thumbnails' MAME repository, and the LaunchBox Games Database for The House of the Dead 3.
